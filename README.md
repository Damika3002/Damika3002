## Who am I?

👋 **Hi, I'm Damika Akalanka!**

I'm a passionate engineering enthusiast who thrives on innovation, creation, and development. Whether it's repairing electronics, coding, or exploring new projects, I enjoy learning and turning ideas into real solutions.

Currently, I'm a full-time **Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at ICBT Campus**. In my spare time, I work on repairs, practical electronics projects, and new technical ideas. Coding is another passion of mine, and I enjoy bringing ideas to life through technology.

When I'm not working on engineering projects, you'll find me pursuing my hobby of racing—modifying motors, tuning engines, and enjoying high-speed rides. 🏍️💨
